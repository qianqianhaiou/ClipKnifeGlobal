# ClipKnife — AI Media Management and Search for Windows

**English** | [简体中文](./README-zh.md)

> Find the shot you remember. Put your media back to work.

**ClipKnife (素刀) is a Windows desktop app for local media asset management and AI-powered search.** It helps creators organize images, videos and audio across folders and drives, find images and video shots using natural-language descriptions or a reference image, and locate spoken content after transcription.

For video editors, content creators and anyone with a growing media collection, ClipKnife makes existing material easier to find, review and reuse in the next project.

[Official website — International](https://clipknife.net/) · [Official website — China](https://clipknife.cn/)

## What problems does ClipKnife solve?

| The problem | How ClipKnife helps |
| --- | --- |
| You remember the scene, but not the filename. | Describe what you remember to search for relevant images and video shots in your own library. |
| Media is scattered across folders and drives. | Bring images, videos and audio into one searchable library without reorganizing every folder by hand. |
| Reviewing long videos takes too much time. | Browse video shots, preview relevant moments and export selected clips for further editing. |
| You remember something that was said in a recording. | Search transcribed speech to locate relevant sections of audio or video. Enable transcription and let it finish first. |
| You have a visual reference but need matching material. | Use image similarity search to discover visually related images and video shots in your library. |
| Useful material gets forgotten after a project ends. | Organize assets and shots into groups, making them easier to revisit and reuse. |

## From a media collection to your next edit

1. **Add your media.** Choose folders or drives containing your images, videos and audio.
2. **Let the library prepare.** Wait until the media is ready to search. Enable transcription if you want to search spoken content.
3. **Describe it or use a reference image.** Find relevant pictures, video shots or transcribed passages.
4. **Review and reuse.** Preview the original media, collect useful results into groups and export selected video clips for your editing workflow.

### Search by what you remember

Instead of guessing a filename, try a description such as:

- “Sunset over the sea, with waves against the rocks.”
- “A close-up of a red product package.”
- “Sunlight coming through trees onto a path.”

These are examples of how to describe a scene. Results depend on the material available in your library and its search readiness; review matches before using them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can ClipKnife search local videos by their visual content?

Yes. ClipKnife lets you describe a scene or use a reference image to find relevant video shots in a prepared local library. You can preview the matching moments to decide whether they fit your project. AI search can miss details or return imperfect matches.

### Can ClipKnife find words spoken in audio or video?

Yes. After you enable the relevant transcription option and processing finishes, you can search the transcribed speech and locate associated time ranges. This is useful when revisiting interviews, narration and other spoken recordings.

### Is ClipKnife a video editor?

ClipKnife focuses on finding, organizing, previewing and reusing media. It supports the preparation stage of an editing workflow; use your video editor to assemble, finish and export the final video.

### Does ClipKnife upload my media?

Core local library preparation, search, video shot processing and transcription do not upload original media to ClipKnife's online service. Optional external AI services may receive selected content, and optional local-network sharing transfers media between authorized devices. Activation, license checks, updates and other online product services require a network connection. See the official documentation for the data scope of each feature.

### Which operating systems does ClipKnife support?

ClipKnife is available for Windows x64. Check the official website for current system requirements, regional availability and optional feature requirements.
